League Metric Glossary

ETEW% (Every Team Every Week): Calculate your win percentage if you played every other team in the league every single week. Formula: $\frac{\text{Outscored Teams}}{(N - 1) \times \text{Weeks Played}}$.

WABL (Wins Above League Level): Difference between actual matchup wins and expected ETEW wins. Positive numbers denote bad luck; negative numbers denote a lucky schedule.

RABL (Rank Above League Level): Difference between ETEW Rank and Standings Rank. $+2$ means your true scoring rank is 2 spots higher than your head-to-head record shows.

AVG*: Trimmed average weekly points, excluding your single highest and single lowest score of the season.

SDEV: Sample standard deviation of weekly scores, measuring scoring consistency across weeks.
