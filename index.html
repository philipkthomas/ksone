League Metric Glossary

ETEW% (Every Team Every Week): Win percentage if playing every team each week. Formula: $\frac{\text{Outscored Teams}}{(N - 1) \times \text{Weeks Played}}$.

WABL (Wins Above League Level): Matchup wins minus ETEW expected wins. Positive indicates tough schedule luck.

RABL (Rank Above League Level): ETEW rank minus actual rank. $+2$ means true scoring strength is 2 spots higher than standings.

AVG*: Trimmed average score excluding your single highest and single lowest score.

SDEV: Sample standard deviation of weekly scores.
