League Metric Glossary

ETEW% (Every Team Every Week): Win percentage if playing every team each week.

WABL (Wins Above League Level): Matchup wins minus ETEW expected wins.

RABL (Rank Above League Level): ETEW rank minus actual rank.

AVG*: Trimmed average score excluding highest and lowest week.

SDEV: Sample standard deviation of weekly scores.
